Associate Professor in Finance

Welcome to my webpage. I am an Associate Professor in Finance in the University of Sussex Business School at the University of Sussex.

Previously, I was a Lecturer at the University of Essex and a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Waterloo. I obtained my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Birmingham in December 2017.

I am a financial econometrician by training. My research interests span financial econometrics, empirical asset pricing, and defi.
